# Hands-on Lab 2 - Executive Briefing Challenge

## Inputs
WinTrust_CEO_Strategy_Briefing_Source.docx
WinTrust_GenAI_Security_Guidelines.docx
WinTrust_Malicious_Operations_Note.docx

## Task
Create:
1. a one-page executive briefing,
2. a version for either the CDO/Data audience or COO/Enterprise Ops audience,
3. a challenge analysis,
4. three questions an executive should ask next.

## Required sections
Executive Message | What Changed | Material Risks | Opportunities | Decisions Required | Open Questions

## Rules
Separate evidence from inference. Do not invent facts.
