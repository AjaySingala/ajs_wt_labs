# WinTrust Executive Briefing Draft

## Q3 Business and Technology Update

WinTrust had a mixed quarter. Revenue performance was generally positive, although some business areas did not perform as expected. Customer activity increased during the quarter and the company continues to see opportunities in digital products and data services.

The operations organization handled a high number of incidents during the quarter. Most incidents were resolved within the expected service levels, but there were some important exceptions. Several incidents affected critical customers and some remained open longer than planned.

The data organization also reported a number of data-quality issues. These included missing values, invalid product codes, inconsistent currency values and duplicate records. The impact of these issues is not fully understood yet, but they may affect reporting and downstream analytics.

From a technology perspective, the organization is increasing its use of AI tools. Teams are using Microsoft Copilot and GitHub Copilot for research, writing, analysis and software development. Early feedback has been positive and productivity appears to be improving, although the benefits have not yet been measured consistently across teams.

There are also some risks. The growing use of AI could create security and governance concerns if employees use AI without following the appropriate controls. There are also concerns around data quality and operational resilience. These areas should be monitored closely.

## Business Performance

Sales activity remained healthy during the quarter. Pipeline levels were strong and several opportunities progressed through the sales process. However, pipeline quality varies between business units and some opportunities have uncertain close dates.

Customer retention remains an important priority. There were some customer complaints during the quarter, particularly relating to service responsiveness. Management should continue to monitor this area.

## Operations

The operations team processed a significant volume of tickets. The majority were resolved successfully. A smaller number of incidents required escalation.

Some incidents involving critical customers exceeded their expected SLA. The reasons included extended investigation times, dependency on other teams and delays in escalation.

Management should consider improving incident monitoring and escalation processes. Additional automation could potentially help identify SLA risks earlier.

## Data and Analytics

The data team identified several quality issues in operational and transaction data.

The main categories were:

- Missing customer information
- Invalid product codes
- Incorrect or unsupported currency values
- Duplicate records
- Inconsistent dates
- Negative or unexpected transaction amounts

These issues could reduce confidence in management reporting and analytics. The organization should improve data-quality monitoring and establish clearer ownership for remediation.

## AI Adoption

AI adoption is increasing across WinTrust.

Current use cases include:

- Executive research and summarization
- Document analysis
- Data analysis
- Software development assistance
- Code explanation and testing
- Drafting business communications

Microsoft Copilot and GitHub Copilot are currently the most visible tools in the program.

Employees generally report that the tools save time. However, there is not yet a consistent measurement framework for productivity, quality, risk or cost.

Management should establish a common measurement approach before making broad claims about business value.

## Key Risks

The most important risks currently appear to be:

1. Operational incidents affecting critical customers
2. Data-quality problems affecting reporting
3. Increasing dependence on technology and digital services
4. Security and governance risks associated with AI adoption
5. Lack of consistent measurement of AI business value

The overall risk position appears manageable, but several areas require continued attention.

## Opportunities

There are opportunities to improve performance through:

- Earlier identification of operational incidents
- Better data-quality monitoring
- Greater use of automation for repetitive operational activities
- More structured adoption of AI tools
- Improved measurement of productivity and business outcomes

Management should prioritize opportunities that have measurable business impact rather than simply increasing technology adoption.

## Recommended Actions

Management should consider the following actions:

1. Strengthen monitoring of critical customer incidents and SLA performance.
2. Establish clearer ownership for major data-quality issues.
3. Define an enterprise approach for responsible AI usage.
4. Establish KPIs for measuring AI productivity and business value.
5. Identify operational processes where controlled automation could reduce manual effort.

## Management Discussion Points

The following questions may require management discussion:

- Are current operational SLA targets appropriate for critical customers?
- Which data-quality issues have the greatest financial or customer impact?
- How should WinTrust measure the value generated by Copilot and GitHub Copilot?
- Which operational processes are suitable for controlled automation?
- What additional governance is required as AI adoption increases?

## Draft Conclusion

WinTrust continues to make progress in business performance, digital adoption and AI-enabled productivity. At the same time, operational reliability, data quality and AI governance require continued management attention.

The immediate priority should be to improve visibility, establish stronger controls and focus investment on areas where measurable business outcomes can be demonstrated.

This briefing is a working draft and should be validated against the underlying business, operational and financial data before being used for an executive decision.
